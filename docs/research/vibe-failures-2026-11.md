# What breaks in vibe-coded apps after launch, and what can be checked from outside

Research for ticket 52 (map ticket 50, feeds ticket 59 and the Grade 1 check list). Written 2026-11-03.

**Detectability key.** **Outside** = the running app plus its served HTML/JS bundle and the public endpoints they name, with no repo and no provider access. **Outside (test account)** = outside, but needs one or two throwaway accounts on the app. **Outside (write)** = provable only with a write or a costly call, so it is safe only against Kahf Labs' test environment or with tight limits. **Repo** = needs source. **Provider** = needs DB, Firebase, Stripe or cloud console access.

**Ranking.** Frequency comes from measured studies where they exist (Escape, Palmer, env.fail, Cybernews, Truffle, Tenzai). Impact means data loss, account takeover or money. Rows marked *unsourced* have no primary measurement of how often they happen. They are ranked by impact and by the vendor docs only.

**Rejected or below the top 10**
- **Float money** (`0.1 + 0.2`): no primary incident or study ties it to vibe-coded apps (*unsourced*). It is detectable only from the Repo, or from Outside by a test purchase. ASVS 5.0 has no precision requirement; the CWE is CWE-1339. Keep it as a Repo-only Grade 2 lint, not Grade 1.
- **Exposed `.git` / source maps**: cheap to check from Outside and named at L1 (ASVS 13.4.1, CWE-540). No vibe-specific frequency data (*unsourced*). Worth adding as a free check.

## Findings that matter for the Grade 1 list

1. Rows 1–4 and 7 can all be found from **outside with read-only requests**, and they cover the studied incidents with the most impact (CVE-2025-48757, Moltbook, Tea, env.fail, Base44, Truffle). Researchers used the same method each time: read the bundle, pull out the config or keys, and replay the calls without auth [P1, W2, F1, E1].
2. **ASVS 5.0 Level 1 does not cover two Grade 1 goals.** Secret management (13.3.1) and anti-automation and cost control (2.4.1) are Level 2. "Don't run up a $10k bill" therefore has to cite API4:2023 and LLM10:2025, not ASVS L1.
3. Proving rows 6, 7 and 9 needs writes or paid calls. Grade 1 should flag their *preconditions* from outside (a writable-looking field, an open LLM or SMS route, a webhook route that exists) and prove them only against a test environment the owner names.
4. Tenzai's frequency figures come only from secondary write-ups, and they conflict on rate limiting. Treat rows 5, 7 and 8 frequencies as provisional until the original post is read.
